Common questions

What are good birthday gifts for someone who has everything?

A night or a moment, not more stuff. Concert tickets, a cooking class, or a movie night for two make a memory; a star map print marks the date itself.

How much should I spend on a birthday gift?

Any amount here can work. Under $25 covers small, fun picks; $25 to $50 covers most shared gifts and games. Spend more only on a big night out or a gift that marks a big year.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

Every idea is a type, not a brand. Most picks carry a price-check link that opens a shopping search for the pick's name. The site sells nothing and earns nothing from the link. This site's one job is good ideas.
