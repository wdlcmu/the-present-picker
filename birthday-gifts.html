Common questions

What are good birthday gifts for someone who has everything?

Give them a night or a moment, not more stuff. Concert tickets, a cooking class, or a movie night for two all make a memory. A star map print marks the date itself.

How much should I spend on a birthday gift?

Any amount on this page can work. Under $25 covers small, fun picks. From $25 to $50 covers most shared gifts and games. Spend more only on a big night out or a gift that marks a big year.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

No. Every idea is described in plain words, with no store links. Search the name anywhere you like to buy it. The site sells nothing, so it has exactly one job: good ideas.
