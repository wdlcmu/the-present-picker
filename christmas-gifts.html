Common questions

What are good Christmas gifts for someone who is hard to buy for?

Pick something warm, tasty, or shared. A candle trio makes the house feel ready. A spice subscription keeps giving after Christmas morning. A heated throw gets used all winter.

How much should I spend on Christmas gifts?

Any amount on this page can work. Under $25 covers small, kind picks. From $25 to $50 covers most everyday gifts. Spend more only on something they will use or keep for years.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

No. Every idea is described in plain words, with no store links. Search the name anywhere you like to buy it. The site sells nothing, so it has exactly one job: good ideas.
