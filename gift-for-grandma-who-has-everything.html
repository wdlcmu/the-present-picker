Common questions

What can I get my grandma who has everything?

Nothing that adds clutter. Pick a gift that gets used up, an upgrade to something she uses daily, or a keepsake that holds the family. The updateable photo frame and the star-map print work because they carry people she loves.

What if my grandma says she wants nothing?

Believe the feeling, not the words: she means no clutter and no fuss. A small, warm gift still lands: the candle trio, a good novel, or seeds for spring.
