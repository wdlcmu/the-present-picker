Is $25 to $50 enough for a gift that lasts?

Yes. A good pan, a warm scarf, or a strong tool can last for years at this price. Pick the one they will use each week, not the one that looks biggest in the box.

Stores set real prices, and prices change. Every idea here is in the $25 to $50 budget band in the picker. Check the tag when you buy, and pick another idea here if one runs over.
