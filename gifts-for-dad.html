Common questions

What are good gifts for a dad who says he wants nothing?

Something he will use, not shelve. A meat thermometer, a headlamp, or a good pan gets used every week. Or give time out: a parks pass, a day outdoors together.

How much should I spend on a gift for Dad?

Any amount here can work. Under $25 covers small, useful picks; $25 to $50 is the sweet spot for most dads. Spend more only when he will use it often.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?
