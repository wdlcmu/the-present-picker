Common questions

What are good gifts for a dad who says he wants nothing?

Pick something he will use, not something that sits on a shelf. A meat thermometer, a headlamp, or a good pan gets used every week. Or give him time out: a parks pass or a day outdoors together.

How much should I spend on a gift for Dad?

Any amount on this page can work. Under $25 covers small, useful picks. From $25 to $50 is the sweet spot for most dads. Spend more only when you know he will use it often.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

No. Every idea is described in plain words, with no store links. Search the name anywhere you like to buy it. The site sells nothing, so it has exactly one job: good ideas.
