Common questions

What is a good gift for a friend without making it awkward?

Keep it fun and light. A party game, a hammock, or a small kit for their hobby says you know them. Skip anything that feels like a bill or a chore.

How much should I spend on a gift for a friend?

Any amount on this page can work. Under $25 is right for most friends. From $25 to $50 fits a close friend or a group gift. Spend more only when you are sure they will love it.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

No. Every idea is described in plain words, with no store links. Search the name anywhere you like to buy it. The site sells nothing, so it has exactly one job: good ideas.
