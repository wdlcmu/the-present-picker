Common questions

What is a good gift for a friend without making it awkward?

Keep it fun and light. A party game, a hammock, or a small kit for their hobby says you know them. Skip anything that feels like a bill or a chore.

How much should I spend on a gift for a friend?

Any amount here can work. Under $25 is right for most friends; $25 to $50 fits a close friend or a group gift. Spend more only when you are sure they will love it.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?
