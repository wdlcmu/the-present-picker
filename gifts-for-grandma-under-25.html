Common questions

What do you get a grandma for under $25?

Warm: the story journal, the family recipe book, the letter kit, all carrying the family. Useful: the book light, the shower speaker, the travel organizer, all reached for daily. Both sets here stay under $25.

Is $25 enough for a good grandma gift?

Yes. At this budget, thought does the work that money does not. A pressed-flower bookmark picked for her garden beats a generic gift set twice its price. Every pick here was checked to feel given, not cheap.

What if my grandma says she does not want anything?

She means no clutter. Pick something used up, like the candle trio or the seed collection, or something that holds the family, like the memory jar. Her has-everything page, linked above, goes deeper.
