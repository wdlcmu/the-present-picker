Common questions

What are good gifts for grandparents who have everything?

Pick comfort, family, or a reason to look outside. A photo frame the family can update, a warm throw, or a bird feeder with a camera all give them something new to enjoy.

How much should I spend on a gift for a grandparent?

Any amount on this page can work. Under $25 covers kind, useful picks. From $25 to $50 covers most comfort gifts. Spend more only when the whole family will enjoy it with them.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

No. Every idea is described in plain words, with no store links. Search the name anywhere you like to buy it. The site sells nothing, so it has exactly one job: good ideas.
