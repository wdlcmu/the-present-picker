Common questions

What is a good $50 to $100 gift for a kid?

A tool for something they already love: a camera for the kid who takes pictures, a microscope for the kid who collects bugs, an easel for the kid who draws every day. Match the habit and the gift earns its price.

Is one big gift better than a few small ones?

It depends on the kid. A kid with a strong hobby uses one right tool all year. A kid who loves new things may like a few small picks more. The under $25 page, linked above, holds the small set.

How do I know it will not sit in a closet?

Match the habit, not the holiday. Every pick on this page says who it fits in its notes. Read the why line on each card: if it sounds like your kid, it will get used.

No. Every idea is described in plain words, with no store links. Search the name anywhere you like to buy it. The site sells nothing, so it has exactly one job: good ideas.
