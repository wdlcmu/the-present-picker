Common questions

What is a good gift for a kid over $100?

A big gift that fits the stage they are in right now: a balance bike for the kid learning to ride, a telescope for the kid who looks up, a play kitchen for the kid who helps with dinner. At this price, the match matters more than the size.

Is it worth spending over $100 on a kid gift?

Only when the gift will be used for months or years, not days. Every pick on this page is built to last past the holiday: solid wood, sturdy frames, and sets that grow with the kid. If the use would be short, the $50 to $100 page, linked above, is the safer spend.

How do I know they will not outgrow it fast?

Look for the grow-with-them signs in the buying notes on each card: an adjustable seat, room to add pieces, a set that starts simple and gets harder. Those are the gifts that stay in use longest.
