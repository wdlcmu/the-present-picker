Common questions

What is a good gift for a kid under $25?

Something they can start the same day: an art kit, picture books, a science kit with pieces to hold. Every pick here opens and works at once, with no extra parts and no shopping trip first.

Are gifts under $25 good enough for Christmas?

For a kid, yes. What lands is the doing, not the price: dough to squish, a hunt to run, a kit to open. A small gift that fits beats a big gift that misses.
