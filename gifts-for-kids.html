Common questions

What are good gifts for kids who already have too many toys?

Pick things they can make, build, or do. Art kits, science kits, and building sets get used for months. A museum membership turns into many rainy day trips.

How much should I spend on a gift for a kid?

Any amount here can work. Under $25 covers art, books, and small kits; $25 to $50 covers most games and big craft sets. Spend more only on gifts that grow with them.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

Every idea is a type, not a brand. Most picks carry a price-check link that opens a shopping search for the pick's name. The site sells nothing and earns nothing from the link. This site's one job is good ideas.
