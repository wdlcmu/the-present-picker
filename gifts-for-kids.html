Common questions

What are good gifts for kids who already have too many toys?

Pick things they can make, build, or do. Art kits, science kits, and building sets get used for months. A museum membership turns into many rainy day trips.

How much should I spend on a gift for a kid?

Any amount on this page can work. Under $25 covers art, books, and small kits. From $25 to $50 covers most games and big craft sets. Spend more only on gifts that grow with them.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

No. Every idea is described in plain words, with no store links. Search the name anywhere you like to buy it. The site sells nothing, so it has exactly one job: good ideas.
