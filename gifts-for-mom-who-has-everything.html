Common questions

What can I get my mom who has everything?

Used up, used daily, or full of family: the same rule as grandma. An experience together, a keepsake with a real date, or a small luxury she would never buy herself beats one more shelf object.

Are experience gifts better for someone who has everything?

Often, yes. A shared plan gives her a date to look forward to, not a thing to store. If you pick an experience from this page, put the date on the calendar before you wrap the card.
