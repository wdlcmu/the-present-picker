Common questions

What are good gifts for a mom who says she wants nothing?

Pick something warm or useful that she would not buy herself. A heated throw, a good pan, or a photo book of the year all land well. Small and thoughtful beats big and random.

How much should I spend on a gift for Mom?

Any amount on this page can work. Under $25 covers small, kind picks. From $25 to $50 covers most everyday upgrades. Spend more only on something she will use or keep for years.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

No. Every idea is described in plain words, with no store links. Search the name anywhere you like to buy it. The site sells nothing, so it has exactly one job: good ideas.
