Common questions

What is a good gift for a neighbor?

Something small that gets used up or used daily: a candle trio, good olive oil, seeds for spring. It says thanks without asking for shelf space, which is the right size for a neighbor gift.

How much should you spend on a neighbor gift?

Under $25 covers most of the street, and the first four picks here sit in that band. Save the $25 to $50 picks for the neighbor who waters your plants or takes your packages in.

What about a neighbor you barely know?

Stay edible or seasonal: the candle trio and the hot sauce trio need no taste guess beyond sweet or heat. Add a card with your name and house number; that is the part they keep.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

Every idea is a type, not a brand. Most picks carry a price-check link that opens a shopping search for the pick's name. The site sells nothing and earns nothing from the link. This site's one job is good ideas.
