Common questions

What is a good gift for a partner when we said no gifts?

Pick a shared night or a small thing that proves you listen. Concert tickets, a cooking class, or a jar of short notes all work. The thought is the gift.

How much should I spend on a gift for my partner?

Any amount on this page can work. Under $25 covers sweet, small picks. From $25 to $50 covers most fun ideas. Spend more on time together or on the one thing they keep mentioning.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

No. Every idea is described in plain words, with no store links. Search the name anywhere you like to buy it. The site sells nothing, so it has exactly one job: good ideas.
