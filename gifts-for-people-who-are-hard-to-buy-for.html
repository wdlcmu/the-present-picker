Common questions

What do you buy someone who is hard to buy for?

First name the kind of hard. Far away: light and personal. Just moved: useful. Wants nothing: a better version of a daily tool. Has everything: used up, used daily, or full of family. Each set here answers one kind.

Are gift cards okay for someone hard to buy for?

As a last resort, yes, but a card says you gave up. One specific idea from their set above, even a small one, lands warmer. If you do give a card, pair it with one small real thing.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

No. The site sells nothing and links to no stores. Search any idea's name anywhere you like to buy it. This site's one job is good ideas.
