Common questions

What is a good gift for a teacher?

Something small and useful they would not buy themselves: a book light for the nightstand stack, good coffee for the morning, a stand that holds a book open at the right angle. Every pick on this page is one of those.

How much should you spend on a teacher gift?

Under $25 is the sweet spot for most class gifts, and the first four picks here sit in that band. A group gift from the whole class can reach for the $50 to $100 picks without any one family spending much.

What should you skip for a teacher?

Skip scented lotions and anything that needs a size or a taste guess. A handwritten note from your child beats both, and it pairs with any pick here.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

Every idea is a type, not a brand. Most picks carry a price-check link that opens a shopping search for the pick's name. The site sells nothing and earns nothing from the link. This site's one job is good ideas.
