Common questions

When should I spend over $100 on a gift?

Only when you know them well and know they will use it. A mixer, good headphones, or a weekend bag can last for years. If you are not sure, pick a smaller idea and add time with them.

Will every idea here cost over $100 in a store?

Stores set real prices, and prices change. Every idea here is in the $100+ budget band in the picker. Check the tag when you buy, and pick another idea here if one runs far over.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

No. Every idea is described in plain words, with no store links. Search the name anywhere you like to buy it. The site sells nothing, so it has exactly one job: good ideas.
