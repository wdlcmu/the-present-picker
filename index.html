How it works

Do you link to stores?

No. Every idea is a type, never linked to a shop. Search the name anywhere you like. The site sells nothing.

How are the five picks chosen?

Your budget is a hard filter. Your interests score each idea. Your style boosts matching picks. Same answers, same order, every time.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no signup and no email. Your saved list lives in your browser only.

Is my list private?

Yes. It is stored on your device and never sent anywhere. Sharing only happens when you tap a share button.
