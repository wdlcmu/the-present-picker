Why I built this

How it works

3 quick answers, plus 2 more only if you want. Five gift ideas.

  1. You answer. 3 quick answers: who it is for, what they are into, what you can spend, plus 2 more only if you want.
  2. We search the 255 checked ideas. Your price range is firm, and no store can pay to rank.
  3. You get five picks. Best match first. Each pick says why it fits, and your list stays in your browser. When a popular gift is wrong for them, we name it, say why, and leave it out.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no signup and no email. Your saved list lives in your browser only.

Is my list private?

Yes. It is stored on your device and never sent anywhere. Sharing only happens when you tap a share button.

What we don't do

We don't sell gifts, and no store can pay to place an idea. Your quiz answers stay in your browser and stay private. The one exception: words on the Ask page (where you describe them in your own words) are read by an AI helper, then discarded. There is nothing to sign up for, and we ask for no email. Every idea comes from our own hand-checked list of 255, ranked for the person.

Works with your AI

Your AI can run this site's gift picker. Ideas come from the same 255 checked gifts, matched to your person and your budget, not ads.

Connect this site to Claude, Cursor, or any other MCP client (MCP, the standard way AI apps connect to outside tools). It is free, and it takes about a minute.

1. Copy the server address

https://mcp.thepresentpicker.com/mcp

2. Add it to your AI

Claude Code: one command 
claude mcp add --transport http present-picker https://mcp.thepresentpicker.com/mcp
Claude Desktop or Cursor: paste into the MCP settings 
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "present-picker": {
      "url": "https://mcp.thepresentpicker.com/mcp"
    }
  }
}
