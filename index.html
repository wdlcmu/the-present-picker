How it works

Do you link to stores?

No. Every idea is described generically, never linked to a shop. Search the name anywhere you like to buy it. The site sells nothing, so it has exactly one job: good ideas.

How are the five picks chosen?

Your budget is a hard filter. Your interests score each idea, and your style gives a boost to matching picks. The order is the same every time for the same answers.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no signup and no email. Your saved list lives in your browser only.

Is my list private?

Yes. It is stored on your device and never sent anywhere. Sharing only happens when you tap a share button.
