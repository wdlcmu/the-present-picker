The Present Picker gift tools

Tell the assistant who the gift is for. Get gift ideas worth giving.

The picks come from a fixed bank. It holds 255 gift ideas. A person picked each one. No brands. No exact prices.

The three tools

  • Pick a gift - up to 5 ideas for one person. They match your budget, likes, and gift kind.
  • Search gift ideas - up to 10 ideas for one interest. Or search one word, like cooking or coffee. It always says how many fit.
  • Get gift details - the full story on one idea. Plus 3 ideas like it.

All three are read-only. They buy nothing, book nothing, and collect nothing.

Try these prompts

"I need a gift for my dad. He likes cooking. I can spend $25 to $50."
"What can I get my grandma? Something warm and sweet, under $25."
"Show me gift ideas for a kid who loves science."
"Search your gift ideas for coffee."
"Tell me more about the cast iron skillet idea."

Good to know

  • Free. No ads. No accounts.
  • Ideas are product types, like "a pour-over coffee set". You buy them wherever you like.
  • Budget is a band, like $25 to $50. Stores set real prices, and prices change.
  • If only 2 ideas fit your exact ask. The tool says so. It does not pad the list.
