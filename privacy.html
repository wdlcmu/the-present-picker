The Present Picker

Privacy

The short version: Your quiz answers stay in your browser. Cloudflare and PostHog count visits here. There are no accounts here.

What the quiz does

You pick a person, interests, and budget.

You may add a first name.

You may add one short description.

The picking math runs in your browser.

Your name and description stay in page memory.

They are never saved or sent.

They clear when you restart the quiz.

The Ask page

One page on this site works in a new way.

That page is the Ask page.

What you type there is read by an AI helper.

The AI helper reads your words.

Then it picks from the same 255 ideas as the quiz.

Nothing you type is saved.

No name is asked.

No contact details are asked.

We count the asks each day, as a plain number.

We count taps on its quiz button too.

Those counts hold no words and no names.

Every other page still runs in your browser.

Your saved list

A list you save stays in your browser.

It holds only gift IDs and person types.

It never reaches us at all.

Tap the remove button to delete one.

Price links

Some picks carry a "See store prices" link.

It opens a shopping search for the pick's name.

The search runs on Bing Shopping.

Prices live at the stores it finds.

The link is a plain search link.

It has no affiliate code and no referral tag.

We earn nothing from the link.

Sharing and prompts

Sharing starts only when you tap Share.

Sharing uses your device share sheet.

Or it copies the text for you.

You choose where the text goes.

The list image is drawn in your browser.

The AI prompt is built in your browser.

You copy it into your own AI.

Site counts

This site loads Cloudflare Web Analytics.

It sends page and performance counts to Cloudflare.

Cloudflare says its beacon uses no cookies.

The home and Ask pages also run PostHog.

PostHog counts page views and quiz starts.

It counts finishes, shares, and return visits.

It counts saved picks, prompts, and images.

PostHog keeps a random ID in your browser.

It tells a first visit from a return visit.

It is not your name or your answers.

Your quiz answers are never sent to it.

It does not record your screen.

Children

Some gifts here are for children.

It is not directed at children under 13.
