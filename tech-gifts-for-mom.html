Common questions

What tech gift do you get a mom who is not into gadgets?

The one that fixes a daily annoyance: the cable set for the drawer of dead cords, the wifi booster for the room that drops calls, the photo frame the family updates for her. Useful beats clever.

What is the best tech gift for a mom under $25?

The fast charging cable set, the phone stand with a charging dock, or the travel adapter. All three sit under $25 on this page, and all three get used the week they arrive.

Will she need help setting it up?

Most picks here work out of the box. The wifi booster and the smart plugs may want ten minutes of setup. Offer that ten minutes with the gift, and it reads as care, not homework.
