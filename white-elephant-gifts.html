Common questions

What makes a white elephant gift actually good?

Two things: it makes the room laugh, and the winner is glad to keep it. Aim for funny plus useful. Pure gag gifts die by January; the hot sauce trio and the harmonica get used all year.

How much do people spend on white elephant gifts?

Most parties set the limit at $20 or $25. Some go to $50 for a big-splash round. Every pick on this page sits inside one of those two bands.

Do you link to stores or sell these gifts?

No. Every idea is described in plain words, with no store links. Search the name anywhere you like to buy it. The site sells nothing, so it has exactly one job: good ideas.
